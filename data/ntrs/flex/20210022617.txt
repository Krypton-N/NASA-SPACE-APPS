JASMAC-33
10/15/2021

o A new phenomenon where a hot flame extinction leads 
to a low-temperature, “cool-flame” burning in 
microgravity has been observed for the first time 

o These cool flames produce partially oxidized fuel and 
potentially re-ignite to hot flames posing fire safety 
concerns in spacecraft environments

o Existing, widely-used detailed chemical kinetic models 
do not accurately predict the 2nd stage cool flame 
extinction (and warm flame) and need further 
improvement

o Improved low temperature chemistry will have wide 
ranging applications including advanced internal 
combustion engine design and development

o The new phenomena may lead to new innovative 
burner designs and fuel reforming technologies

o A wealth of experimental data with a variety of fuels, 
fuel mixtures, and ambient conditions (over 1500 tests)
