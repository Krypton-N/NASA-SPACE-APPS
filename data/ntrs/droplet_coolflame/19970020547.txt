13. ABSTRACT (Maximum 200 words)

This Conference Publication contains 84 papers presented at the Fourth International Microgravity Combustion Workshop
held in Cleveland, Ohio, from May 19 to 21, 1997. The purpose of the workshop was twofold: to exchange information
about the progress and promise of combustion science in microgravity and to provide a forum to discuss which areas in
microgravity combustion science need to be expanded profitably and which should be included in upcoming NASA
Research Announcements (NRA).
