Summary|

• 2-D computer model of concurrent flame spread on cotton using FDS 6.7 is presented.

• The chemistry model and parameters used (mixing controlled vs. finite rate) plays a significant 
role in the spread rate results, leading to blow-off extinction if the appropriate parameters are 
not selected

• Numerical simulations verify that concurrent spread is mainly controlled by convective heat 
transfer. Nevertheless, as pressure or gravity are reduced radiative processes become more 
important when compared to convective effects.

• Increasing the oxygen concentration increases spread rates, but model underpredicts 
normoxic experimental results.

• Burning of solid fuels is a very complex and difficult process to predict. Further work needs to 
be done
