Understanding flame spread in different conditions

• Improve safety procedures, especially in enclosed habitats

• NASA’s interest for future space exploration vehicles:
– low pressure, increased oxygen
– low velocity flows
– different gravity levels

• These environmental conditions affect flame spread and 
material flammability, and can lead to unexpected events

• Testing real fire scenarios in space is not always feasible



Can we mimic low gravity flames by reducing pressure?

• Sub-atmospheric pressures offer an alternative to 
reduced gravity

• Reduced buoyancy in partial gravity, implications on 
heat and mass transfer mechanisms

• How does low pressure affect flame characteristics 
and spread?

Discussion

• Pressure reductions slow down the flames, and make them less bright 
and ”thicker” (reaction zone)

• Anomaly of blue region moving towards fuel surface at low pressures

• Theoretical dependence of flame standoff distance                 close to 
experimental values. Differences could be due to measurement errors 
or additional pressure dependence of the length scale

• The standoff distance for opposed flame spread follows the same trend 
as for concurrent flame spread by varying ambient pressure
