Conclusions
• Tablet fires show an outburst of electrolyte that vents and lasts for 10-15 s followed by a fire
that lasts between 20-40 s
• A total energy release between 24.3- 33.3 kJ and flame heights that reach up to 0.4 m
• Pouch cell surface temperatures reach maximum temperatures between 817-900 K
• Maximum of 2 pouch cells going into thermal runaway
• Surface Pro Tablet with the patch heater ignition method
• Lower tox levels (below SMAC levels), particulate concentrations and obscuration per
foot
• “Worst case” fire demonstrations with the Dell XPS 15 (95 Wh) and the electric coil ignition
method show
• CO surpasses the SMAC thresholds and OSEF 1 hr SMAC requirement
• Higher particulate concentrations
• Visibility exceeds threshold levels for vehicles
